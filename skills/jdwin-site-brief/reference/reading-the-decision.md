# Reading a JDWIN decision

Everything here is what JDWIN states publicly (jdwin.ai, its documentation and its `llms.txt`).

## The index and the four bands

| Index | Decision | Meaning |
|:-:|:-:|---|
| 82 – 100 | **امضِ** · Proceed | A strong band |
| 65 – 81 | **امضِ بحذر** · Proceed with care | An opportunity, conditional on fixing specific points the report names |
| 45 – 64 | **لا تمضِ بعد** · Do not proceed yet | Risk outweighs the opportunity for now |
| below 45 | **لا تمضِ** · Do not proceed | Not advised at this location |

An unconditional «امضِ» is reserved for 82 and above. An index of 81 is «امضِ بحذر», not «امضِ». JDWIN uses these four
words only; there is no second rating vocabulary.

## The four main indicators

- **الطلب والسوق** · demand and market
- **المنافسة والفجوة** · competition and the gap left open
- **الوصول والحركة** · access and movement
- **مستوى المخاطر** · level of risk

Beneath them sit detailed variables such as street class, frontage visibility, proximity to attractors, movement
density, parking, ease of turning, competitive saturation and the negative signal in reviews.

## Sources and honesty

Every report states the source of each data layer and says which layers could not be measured. Where JDWIN has no open
source for a figure (household purchasing power below the region level is one), the value is an estimate and the report
does not present it as measured.

## Stated limits

- Execution and management quality are not measured.
- Sudden events are not predicted: a regulatory change, a new competitor, an infrastructure project.
- Financial figures are a guiding range, not a commitment.
- Accuracy varies with how much data an area has.

## How to explain a result

1. Name the band and read its meaning aloud.
2. Point to the indicator that pulled the index down the most, in the report's own words.
3. Name what the report says it could not measure.
4. Offer the next one or two requests worth testing (a nearby street, another format), each run on its own.
